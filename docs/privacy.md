# Privacy policy

Chiptune Forge collects no personal data. This policy covers the app on
every platform (Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux and the web) and its
command line.

- **No data leaves your device.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no
  advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. It makes no network
  requests. The Android app does not ask for the internet permission.
- **What it stores, on your device only:** your library of saved songs and
  sounds, your settings, and any community packs you add. Uninstalling the
  app (or clearing the site's data, on the web) deletes them.
- **Files you export** are saved where you choose, or downloaded by your
  browser on the web. Files you open, such as a pack, are read and nothing
  else.
- **Links.** "Find and make packs" opens the project's page on GitHub in
  your browser. GitHub's own privacy policy applies there.
- **Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from
  anyone.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/xsoc/chiptune-forge/issues.

Last updated: 17 November 2026.
